MODNet photographic portrait matting model

This directory contains the MODNet photographic ONNX model, derived from the MODNet model
released by the MODNet authors and converted to ONNX by yakhyo. MODNet performs learned
portrait alpha matting; it is not a general semantic background segmenter.
